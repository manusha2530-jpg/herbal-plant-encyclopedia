🌿 Why learn about herbal plants?

Herbal plants are part of traditional knowledge, food culture, gardening and biodiversity. Learning about them helps us appreciate nature and understand the importance of responsible plant use.

🔎 Learn before you use

Traditional use does not prove that a plant treats or cures a disease. Correct identification, dosage, preparation and safety matter. Some herbs can cause side effects or interact with medicines.

Herbal Plant Growing Guide

Basic gardening tips for growing healthy plants.

☀️
Sunlight

Check the light requirements of each plant. Some herbs prefer full sunlight while others grow well in partial shade.

💧
Watering

Water according to the plant and soil condition. Avoid keeping roots constantly waterlogged.

🪴
Healthy Soil

Use suitable, well-draining soil and containers with drainage holes where needed.

🌱
Propagation

Depending on the species, plants may grow from seeds, cuttings, bulbs or divisions.

✂️
Pruning

Remove damaged leaves and trim plants carefully using clean gardening tools.

🐝
Biodiversity

Encourage pollinators and use responsible, low-impact gardening practices.

🌳 Start a small herbal garden

Choose plants suitable for your climate and available space. Begin with a few familiar herbs, label each plant correctly and learn its care requirements before expanding your garden.

Herbal Safety Awareness

Natural does not always mean safe.

🛡️ Use herbal knowledge responsibly

Herbal plants can contain active substances. Safety depends on the exact species, plant part, preparation, amount, health conditions and medicines a person takes. Do not use a plant as a substitute for professional medical care.

🔍
Correct Identification

Some plants look alike. Confirm the plant's identity before handling or using it.

💊
Medicine Interactions

Herbal products may interact with prescription or over-the-counter medicines.

🤰
Pregnancy & Children

Extra caution is needed. Do not assume herbal products are safe for everyone.

⚠️
Allergies

Plants and plant products may cause allergic reactions or skin irritation.

🏥
Medical Advice

Ask a qualified healthcare professional before using herbs for health purposes.

🌱
Responsible Harvesting

Avoid unknown plants and do not collect protected or endangered species.
